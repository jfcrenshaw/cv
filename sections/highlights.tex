\section{Research Highlights}
% Results that show the program's arc: survey design, science case, first data, new science.
% Each should be a result with a consequence that appears nowhere else in the CV, on one line.
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=0pt]
    \item \textbf{Survey design:} My LBG studies motivated a 40\% increase in LSST $u$-band observing time.
    \item \textbf{Science case:} My forecasts show LBGs break key degeneracies in LSST's low-redshift cosmology, improving its neutrino-mass constraint 5.6$\times$ and dark-energy figure of merit 2.6$\times$.
    \item \textbf{First data:} I am leading DESC's first LBG analyses, finding $>$9,000 candidates per deg$^2$ in Rubin DP2.
    \item \textbf{New science:} Rubin's first CGM dust measurement, probing dust-rich halos around low-mass galaxies.
\end{itemize}
